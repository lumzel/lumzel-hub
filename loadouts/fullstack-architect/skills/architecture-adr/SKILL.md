# Architecture Decision Record (ADR) Guide

When proposing or documenting an architectural decision:

1. **Context**: State the concrete problem, constraints, and forces at play in 2-4 sentences.
2. **Options Considered**: Compare at least 2 realistic alternatives with clear trade-offs.
3. **Decision**: State the chosen path and why it wins under current constraints.
4. **Consequences**: List what becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what invariants must be maintained.
