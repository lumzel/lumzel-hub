---
name: compound-knowledge
description: Core rule of Compound Engineering—each unit of engineering work should leave the codebase easier to understand and change next time.
---

- **80/20 Leverage**: Invest heavily in upfront codebase research (`compound-plan`) and multi-angle review (`compound-review`) so implementation is straightforward and clean.
- **Reuse Existing Idioms**: Match the repository's established naming, error handling, and state patterns before introducing new ones.
- **Codify Non-Obvious Fixes**: Whenever you uncover a subtle root cause or architectural invariant, capture it (`compound-codify`) so future sessions never repeat the same investigation.
